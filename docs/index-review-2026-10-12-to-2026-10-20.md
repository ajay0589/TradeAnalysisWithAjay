# Index Scanner Review October 12 to October 20 2026

The current index signals have not demonstrated a profitable edge in this sample.
There are 24 positions: 23 closed and one open. All five winners entered on
October 16. Stop sizing and exit-monitoring evidence need attention before adding
more confirmation filters or treating a high score as reliable performance.

## Scope and Reconciliation

Sources supplied by the user:
- `index-trades-2026-10-12-to-2026-10-20.csv`
- `index-scan-log-2026-10-12-to-2026-10-20.zip`

All 24 CSV trade IDs, entry prices and statuses match the ZIP records. Signed
gross points were independently recalculated from entry, exit and direction.
The archive was generated at 08:38:17 IST on October 20, before that day's session.
Retained scanner activity covers October 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 and 19.

The export includes positions overlapping its dates, not only entries during the
period. One NIFTY positional trade entered October 1 and exited October 15. Records
show their latest stored state, not a reconstruction of each day's ledger.

## Findings

### Stop and backtest mismatch

Live `_stop_level` in `trading_analysis/nifty/live_scanner.py` chooses the nearest
valid support/resistance first. Its legacy `_backtest_stop` instead uses the
larger of ATR and fallback percentage. Therefore that backtest does not reproduce
the same stop policy as these live index signals.

Five NIFTY intraday entries have stops under 0.25 ATR. One has only 3.65 points
of risk against ATR 52.21; another stopped out after 13 seconds with 5.45 points
of risk. Three of those five tight-stop examples won, so widening every stop is
not a proven fix. Compare structural stops with volatility and execution-cost
floors using identical live/replay rules and an untouched validation period.

### Exit-monitoring anomaly

The October 14 Bank Nifty positional short entered at 55,316.00, with a stop of
55,499.65. It exited October 15 at 13:30:47 for 56,029.75, a loss of -3.886R.
The 10:24:54 scan already contains a completed candle close of 55,720.70, beyond
that stop. Run records continue through the morning; this is not evidence of a
multi-hour scanner computation delay.

The packet lacks a continuous quote-exit heartbeat and rejection reasons for that
position. It cannot establish whether earlier fresh quotes were unavailable,
monitoring was interrupted, or stored history was imported/reconciled later.
The NIFTY legacy positional trade also exited at 13:30:47 beyond its stop.

Audit open-trade identity, instance ownership, quote freshness and exit-check
results before attributing these losses only to technical entry quality. The
NIFTY `open_checked` counter increments only when an outcome exists; a zero is
not proof that the repository held no open positions.

### Options and scores

Among the 23 closed trades, the stored rolling option premium-flow direction
agreed with entry for ten (three wins), conflicted for five (two wins), and was
unavailable/unrecorded for eight (no wins). This retrospective grouping is not a
replay of trades that a changed filter would create or suppress.

Aggregate OI bias, premium flow and individual windows can disagree. Keep those
states separate; an OI classification is not verified institutional intent. The
13 closed trades scored 95 include five winners and eight losers: 95 is not a
95% win probability. India VIX at entry is recorded for only four closed trades,
so this packet cannot support meaningful VIX-regime comparisons.

### Runtime and alerts

- Bank Nifty: 6,880 completed runs; median one second, 95th percentile one second,
  maximum five seconds. Sensex: 6,883 completed runs; median zero seconds at the
  stored timestamp resolution, 95th percentile one second, maximum five seconds.
- NIFTY opportunity scans: 2,099 completed; median one second, 95th percentile
  four seconds, maximum 12 seconds. Run completion does not itself prove data
  freshness or successful independent quote-exit monitoring.
- Intraday signal detection delays range from 7.259 to 58.317 seconds, with a
  median of 9.630 seconds. These are measured from candle close, not Telegram
  delivery. Several positional references were recorded after the regular close.
- Telegram: 45 events marked sent, one NIFTY exit marked `delivery_unknown`
  after an eight-second read timeout. Unknown is not confirmed non-delivery;
  automatic resending could duplicate it. Confirmed delivery latency has median
  0.765 seconds and maximum 9.022 seconds.
- One IV-recording job failed with permission denied reading the NIFTY 15-minute
  CSV. Concurrent replacement, file locking or sync software needs checking;
  this message alone does not establish which caused it.
- Many repeated checks reject technical direction, constituent confirmation or
  option alignment. Their counts are not distinct missed trading opportunities.
