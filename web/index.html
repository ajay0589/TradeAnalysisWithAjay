How risk/reward and result quality are measured
1R riskEntry minus the initial stop. The default stop is the lower of Candle 1 and Candle 2 lows.
Target RAt 2R, the target distance is twice the initial risk. Yellow-line exit can close the simulation earlier.
Average RAverage result measured in units of initial risk. Above 0 means positive historical expectancy after configured costs.
Profit factorGross historical gains divided by gross historical losses. Above 1 means gains exceeded losses in the selected sample.
Break-even win rateFor a pure 2R target and 1R loss it is 33.33%; actual break-even differs when yellow or time exits occur.
Evidence checkReview expectancy, drawdown, at least 30 trades, profile consistency, costs, and out-of-sample dates together.
